# 🔬 NeuRo-Lab VINS Research Group

### Hacettepe University | Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Welcome to the official organization of the Visual-Inertial Navigation Systems (VINS) research group at **NeuRo-Lab (Neural Robotics Laboratory)**.

Our laboratory operates within the **Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Hacettepe University**, research in robotics, autonomous systems, and sensor fusion under the supervision of **Assoc. Prof. Dr. Ismail Uyanik**.

---

## About The Project

This organization serves as the central hub for our work on **Visual-Inertial Navigation Systems (VINS)**. Our research focuses on developing and refining algorithms that enable robotic platforms to navigate autonomously with high precision.

### Key Objectives
We aim to achieve robust state estimation and mapping by fusing data from:
*  **Visual Sensors:** Stereo Cameras
*  **Inertial Sensors:** IMU (Inertial Measurement Units)
